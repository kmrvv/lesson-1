Mission 9 • Super Grammar

👑 The Mighty Verb: TO BE (am / is / are)

In English, we use AM, IS, and ARE to say who we are, how old we are, and where we are!

⚡ 3 Superhero Forms of TO BE:

Look at how our new friends from the textbook used them:

I + AM
  • "I am Alex."
  • "I am ten years old."
  • "I am in the UK now."
He / She / It + IS
  • "My name is Jen."
  • "Lucas is from Spain."
  • "My hobby is making cupcakes."
We / You / They + ARE
  • "We are mum and dad."
  • "My hobbies are music and Maths."
  • "The kids are in a club."

⚡ Quick Check: Can you fill in the blanks?

1. I a student.
2. Lucas from Spain.
3. We ready to learn!
